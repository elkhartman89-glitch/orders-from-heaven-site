THE ORIGIN

0RD3RS FR0M H3AVEN is the electronic project of Tommy Sailor — a Navy veteran from South Bend, Indiana, husband, father of four boys, worship leader, and sales account manager.

An ordinary man living an ordinary life. But obedience changes everything.

In a culture that teaches us to create ourselves, to chase impulse, to build identity from noise — this project moves in the opposite direction.

Each release begins in prayer. The direction is not self-generated. It is received.

This is not music for escape. It is music for alignment.

Not rebellion against God — but rebellion against the distortion that keeps us from Him.

Neon meets surrender. Sound becomes instruction.

“Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.” — Romans 12:2
